## pork + cabbage dumplings v3

300g pork mince
200g cabbage, salted + squeezed
20g ginger · 2 spring onions
1 tbsp light soy · 1 tsp sesame oil
60g cold water

Stir water into the pork a little at a time until sticky. Fold in everything else. About 20g filling per wrapper. Pan-fry, add water, cover for 6 minutes, then uncover to crisp.

More ginger than v2. Much better.
